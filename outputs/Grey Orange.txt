# Company Overview
Grey Orange is a technology company that specializes in artificial intelligence and robotics solutions for industries such as e-commerce, retail, and logistics. The company focuses on providing innovative and efficient solutions to enhance supply chain management and warehouse operations.

# CEO
The CEO of Grey Orange is Samay Kohli.

# Headquarters
The headquarters of Grey Orange is located in Singapore, with additional offices in India, the United States, and other countries.

# Industry
Grey Orange operates in the technology and logistics industry, specifically in the fields of artificial intelligence, robotics, and supply chain management.

# Products and Services
Grey Orange offers a range of products and services, including robotic sortation systems, automated warehousing solutions, and artificial intelligence-powered supply chain management software. Their flagship products include the Butler and Sorter robots, designed to streamline warehouse operations and improve efficiency.
